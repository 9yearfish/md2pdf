## Design Context

### Users

People turning Markdown, AI responses, technical notes, reports and contracts into polished PDFs. They want a fast, private tool that works on desktop and mobile without uploading the document.

### Brand Personality

Professional, mechanical and precise at its core, with brief lighthearted details that make direct manipulation feel responsive rather than sterile.

### Aesthetic Direction

An editorial printing-workbench interface: monochrome, square, typographic and tool-like. Motion should extend the existing machinery metaphor with short press, carriage and paper-feed gestures. Playfulness is an accent, never the dominant voice.

### Design Principles

- Keep the workflow fast and legible; motion acknowledges an action but never delays it.
- Preserve the monochrome, square-edged visual system and use movement—not extra colour—for delight.
- Prefer one consistent interaction language across static and dynamically created controls.
- Animate only transforms and opacity, and disable decorative motion for reduced-motion users.
- Keep document privacy explicit: interaction effects and analytics never inspect document content.
